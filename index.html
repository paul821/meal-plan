Why a mini-cut: Evolt scan Nov 6 2026 — 173.9 lb (6 PM, pre-dinner), 19.3% BF, 140.4 lb lean. The "lean bulk" at ~2,600 stalled because real maintenance is ~2,700 (breakfast is ~800, not 540, plus the cheat day). Goal: ~0.5 lb/wk down to ~15–16% BF (~166–168 lb pre-dinner) by early–mid Jan, then a real lean bulk. Rescan mid-Dec, same conditions (pre-dinner, height 5'11").

Sunday cook order · ~45 min
0:00Slice 2 lb thighs, toss in shawarma seasoning. Wok, no oil: sear in 2–3 batches, ~6 min a side. 0:15Microwave the sweet potato mix (1.5 lb total) and the frozen veg in bulk. 0:20Same wok: brown 2 lb of the week's meat — beef + soy/garlic (Week A) or turkey + taco packet (Week B). ~10 min. 0:30Salmon bowls: 1 can + ~⅓ lb sweet potato + 2 tbsp feta + lemon each. 0:35Portion the rest: ½ lb raw-weight meat + frozen veg per container. Microwave a Minute rice cup at eating time (or portion 1 cup in now). ~0:45Done. 12 containers. Dishes: wok + frying pan.
