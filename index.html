Salmon potato bowl

Canned salmon · potatoes · rice · feta

Makes ~3–4 containers · ~660 cal each

Canned salmon 5 oz (3–4 cans)drain + rinse
Baby potatoes (~1.5 cups)microwave 5–6 min
White rice (from big batch)~1 cup/container

Barely any cooking: flake salmon over microwaved potatoes and rice, add veg, feta, lemon. A pre-steamed potato pouch makes it near zero-cook. Rinse the salmon to cut sodium, drizzle olive oil for calories.

Flavor + add-ons · keep separate, hit at eating time
Sauce barChili crisp, sriracha, hot honey, tzatziki / garlic sauce, kewpie. Rotate one per day so the same base tastes different all week.
Kimchi (optional)Great flavor and crunch, but fermented cabbage bloats some people. If it hits you, quick cucumber pickles give the same crunch without the gas.
Pickled onions / picklesJarred acid and crunch that cut through the richer bowls. No prep.
Furikake / everything seasoningShake over the rice for instant flavor at roughly zero calories.
Lactose-free Greek yogurtLazy creamy protein topper. Going lactose-free skips the bloat that regular cottage cheese or yogurt can cause. Spoon it on.
Fresh at serveLemon / lime, avocado (halve when eating), cherry tomatoes, extra spinach. Added at the table, never prepped Sunday.
Sunday cook order · fits in ~60 min
0:00Start the rice cooker with a big batch of white rice (hands-off, feeds the chicken and beef/turkey bowls). 0:03Coat 3 lb chicken thighs in shawarma seasoning. Air fryer batch 1: 400°F, 18–20 min. 0:08Brown the week's ground meat in one pan — beef + gochujang glaze (Week A) or turkey + taco packet (Week B). ~10 min. 0:20Second air-fryer tray of thighs if needed. Microwave the baby potatoes and frozen veg in bulk. 0:25Assemble salmon bowls cold: salmon + microwaved potatoes + rice, add veg, feta, lemon. 0:35Portion everything across containers — protein + ~1.25 cups rice + veggies each. ~1:00Done. Dishes: rice cooker, one pan, air fryer basket. No marinades, no overnight prep.
Staple prep · the two things everything is built on
White riceCook one big batch in the rice cooker Sunday. ~1.25 cups per container is the carb engine across every rotation (the salmon bowl leans on potato plus a smaller scoop of rice).
Frozen mixed veggiesMicrowave from frozen, dump into any container. No washing, no wilting, no Sunday prep. Same bag works for every rotation.
